Channel logic

When Expandi, when HubSpot

The two tools are not interchangeable, and running both at one contact is how you lose them. Four rules decide it.

1 · Lawful basis to email?

Yes → HubSpot leads. It scales, it reports, it costs no sender risk. No → LinkedIn is the only channel and Expandi leads.

2 · Have they ignored email?

Then email is not the answer again. Every "emailed, no response" segment moves to LinkedIn — non-openers only, and at least a week later.

3 · Commercial or informational?

Registrations scale over email. Renewals, revivals and demos convert better in a LinkedIn thread — a reply costs one line instead of a composed email.

4 · Never both in seven days

Enforced by list construction, not timing. EX-08 and HS-08 never share a contact. Nor do EX-04 and HS-03, or EX-06 and HS-05b.
